2. PERSONAL DATA WE MAY PROCESS

Depending on your relationship with 10xTeam, we may process the following categories of personal data:

  • Identification and contact data: name, surname, email address, phone number, signature, job title, company and professional contact information.
  • Account and authentication data: user identifiers, encrypted credentials or authentication tokens, roles, permissions, access logs and security settings.
  • Business and professional data: legal business name, industry, position, company size, goals, processes, commercial preferences and business information provided during onboarding, assessment or support.
  • Tax and billing data: Mexican taxpayer identification number (RFC) or other tax identifiers, legal business name, tax regime, tax address, invoice details and supporting documents.
  • Payment and transaction data: subscribed plan, charges, payment history, payment status and references supplied by payment processors. Full payment card data may be processed directly by specialized providers and is not necessarily stored by 10xTeam.
  • Technical and usage data: IP address, browser, operating system, device or session identifiers, dates and times, logs, pages viewed, usage events, cookies and similar technologies, as well as approximate location inferred from the network when needed for security or analytics.
  • Support and communications data: messages, emails, chats, requests, attachments, comments, surveys and, where prior notice is provided, call recordings or transcripts.
  • Artificial intelligence data: instructions, prompts, files or context entered into AI features, generated outputs, evaluations and user corrections.
  • Derived data: metrics, statistics, patterns and signals obtained from use of the Service, which may be converted into aggregated or de-identified information for analytics and improvement.

Sensitive data. 10xTeam does not generally request sensitive personal data to create an account or contract for the Service. If a particular feature, support request or situation requires its processing, the purpose will be disclosed and the applicable consent obtained under relevant law. The Customer should avoid uploading sensitive data about third parties unless strictly necessary and unless it has the required authorizations.

3. DATA SOURCES

We may obtain personal data: (i) directly from you; (ii) from the company or organization for which you work; (iii) from forms, calendars, assessments, chats, support, events and contracting processes; (iv) from integrations you authorize; (v) from providers supporting the Service; and (vi) from lawful public or professional sources when needed for a commercial relationship or for security.

4. PRIMARY OR NECESSARY PURPOSES

We use personal data for purposes necessary to establish, administer and fulfill our relationship with you or the organization you represent, including:

  • creating, configuring, authenticating, administering and protecting accounts and access;
  • providing, operating, personalizing, maintaining and improving the Service and contracted features;
  • performing onboarding, implementation, migration, support, training and incident management;
  • processing payments, authorized recurring charges, invoicing, taxes, clarifications and administration of the commercial relationship;
  • sending transactional, security, support, billing, contract-change, term-end, renewal, continuation, maintenance or Service communications;
  • preventing fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, security incidents and use contrary to the Terms;
  • enabling integrations, communications, automations and artificial intelligence features requested by the user;
  • measuring performance, quality, stability, usage and operation of the Service;
  • generating analytics, metrics and Derived Data; when used outside the Customer's account, we will seek to reasonably aggregate or de-identify them;
  • complying with legal, regulatory, tax, contractual, retention, audit and defense-of-rights obligations, and responding to competent authorities;
  • addressing privacy rights requests, revocation, restrictions, complaints or inquiries.

5. SECONDARY PURPOSES

Where permitted by applicable law, 10xTeam may use contact and commercial relationship data for secondary purposes such as sending information about products, new features, events, content, promotions or related services; conducting satisfaction surveys and market research; and developing personalized commercial communications based on an existing relationship with 10xTeam.

You may request to stop receiving promotional communications at any time using the unsubscribe link in the message or by writing to hello@10xteam.com.mx. Refusing these secondary purposes will not affect services already contracted.

10xTeam will not publish a Customer's name, testimonial or logo as a success story without prior authorization where required or where the materials clearly identify an individual.

6. CONSENT AND GROUNDS FOR PROCESSING

Processing will take place, as applicable, on the basis of the data subject's consent, performance of a contractual or precontractual relationship, compliance with legal obligations, defense of rights, security measures and other grounds permitted by applicable law. Where express consent is required, it will be requested through a separate affirmative action or equivalent mechanism.

7. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND AUTOMATED PROCESSING

10xTeam may use artificial intelligence tools, statistical models, rules and automated systems to generate content, classify information, summarize conversations, detect patterns, prioritize tasks, make recommendations or assist in carrying out features requested by users.

AI features may process information supplied by the user or available within the user's account to produce the requested result. 10xTeam may use specialized providers as processors or sub-processors without publicly identifying them individually in this Notice, provided they are subject to appropriate confidentiality, security and processing obligations suited to the nature of the service.

Where applicable, the data subject may object to solely automated processing that produces undesired legal effects or significantly affects their interests, rights or freedoms, in accordance with applicable law. AI features must not automatically replace human assessment in sensitive decisions unless an appropriate legal basis and safeguards exist.

8. PROCESSORS, SUB-PROCESSORS AND INTERNATIONAL PROCESSING

To provide the Service, 10xTeam may engage specialized providers acting as processors or sub-processors, including categories such as technology infrastructure and hosting, security, communications, email, telephony, payment processing, analytics, support, integrations, storage and artificial intelligence.

These providers may process information within or outside Mexico and the United States, depending on their infrastructure location and the nature of the service. Where applicable, 10xTeam shall implement contracts, instructions and reasonable safeguards to limit processing to service delivery and applicable legal obligations.

Use of processors or sub-processors does not mean that those providers may freely use the data for their own purposes incompatible with 10xTeam's instructions. Specific information about certain nonpublic providers may be made available where there is a legal obligation or legitimate business need, subject to reasonable confidentiality arrangements.

9. TRANSFERS OF DATA TO THIRD PARTIES

10xTeam does not sell personal data to third parties for monetary consideration.

We may disclose or transfer personal data to third parties acting as independent controllers when necessary to: comply with legal obligations or official requests; protect or exercise rights in legal proceedings; complete a corporate transaction such as a merger, acquisition, reorganization or asset sale; or maintain or fulfill a legal relationship with the data subject, where permitted by law.

10xTeam does not currently transfer data to unrelated third parties for their own direct marketing without consent. If such a transfer requiring consent is contemplated in the future, consent will be requested beforehand and this Notice updated as applicable.

10. COOKIES AND SIMILAR TECHNOLOGIES

10xTeam websites and services may use cookies, local storage, pixels, tags, SDKs and similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, strengthen security, measure performance and analyze site usage.

We may classify these technologies as essential, functional, analytical and, if enabled, advertising-related. Where a law requires prior consent for non-essential technologies, 10xTeam will implement a preference mechanism before activating them.

Users may configure their browsers to block or delete certain cookies, although some features may stop working properly. Where legally applicable, 10xTeam will recognize the opt-out mechanisms or privacy preference signals required in the relevant jurisdiction.

11. SECURITY AND DATA BREACHES

10xTeam implements reasonable technical, administrative and organizational measures to protect personal data against loss, alteration, destruction, use, access or unauthorized processing. These measures are reviewed according to risk, information sensitivity and technological developments.

If 10xTeam confirms a security breach that significantly affects the property or moral rights of data subjects, it will notify affected individuals without undue delay and, where applicable, the competent authority, providing information necessary for them to take protective measures.

12. RETENTION, BLOCKING, DELETION AND DE-IDENTIFICATION

We will retain personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described, maintain the contractual relationship, provide support, resolve disputes, comply with tax, accounting, regulatory and retention obligations, and defend legal rights. Thereafter, data may be blocked, deleted or anonymized according to applicable laws and technical cycles.

Data processed on behalf of a Customer within the Platform will be retained in accordance with the Customer's instructions, the agreement, any data processing agreement, export periods and applicable legal obligations. Backup copies may remain for limited technical cycles before being overwritten.

13. ARCO RIGHTS, REVOCATION AND RESTRICTION

Data subjects in Mexico may exercise their rights of Access, Rectification, Cancellation and Opposition (“ARCO”) at any time, and may request revocation of consent or restrictions on the use or disclosure of their data, where applicable.

Requests should be sent to hello@10xteam.com.mx and include at least: (i) the data subject's name and a means of receiving notices; (ii) documents or reasonably sufficient information to establish identity and, where applicable, representation; (iii) a clear description of the personal data in question; (iv) the specific right or request; and (v) any information helping us locate the data.

10xTeam shall communicate its decision within a maximum of twenty (20) days from receipt of the request and, where the request is granted, shall implement it within fifteen (15) days following communication of the decision. These periods may each be extended once for an equal period when circumstances justify it, in accordance with applicable law.

The exercise of rights is free of charge, except for reproduction or delivery costs allowed by law. 10xTeam may request additional information strictly necessary to verify identity and protect data against fraudulent requests.

14. PRIVACY RIGHTS IN THE UNITED STATES

If you reside in a U.S. state whose privacy law applies to 10xTeam and your relationship with us, you may have additional rights which, depending on the jurisdiction, include: knowing or accessing personal information; correcting it; requesting deletion; obtaining a portable copy; opting out of the sale, sharing or targeted advertising of personal information; limiting certain uses of sensitive information; or appealing a decision on a privacy request.

10xTeam shall not discriminate against an individual for exercising valid privacy rights. Requests may be sent to hello@10xteam.com.mx. Where a state law requires a specific opt-out mechanism, global privacy preference signal or “Privacy Choices” link, 10xTeam shall enable it to the extent that law applies.

10xTeam does not sell personal information for money. If advertising technology used in the future is considered a “sale,” “sharing” or targeted advertising under an applicable state law, the relevant choices will be provided.

15. MINORS

10xTeam services are not intentionally directed to individuals under 18 for direct contracting. If 10xTeam identifies that it collected a minor's data without the required authorization, it shall take reasonable measures to delete it or regularize the processing in accordance with applicable law.
